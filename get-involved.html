Who We Are

Motswedi means "a fountain". For us, it is a fountain of hope.

The Centre is a project of the Mother’s Union of the Dutch Reformed Church in Botswana – Mochudi Congregation. It was officially opened in 1995 and continues to serve people with disabilities through rehabilitation, education and skills training.

Rehabilitation Education Skills Training Physiotherapy Residential Care
What We Do

Services designed to unlock potential

Early Childhood Development

Stimulation, self-care, independence, pre-writing, pre-numeracy and pre-reading.

Vocational Training

Horticulture, cookery, housekeeping, carpentry, dressmaking, poultry and handicrafts.

Physiotherapy

Assessment, treatment, referrals, appliances and counselling for clients and caregivers.

Community Outreach

Information, education, counselling, home adaptation and stakeholder referrals.

Group Therapy

Caregiver support through counselling, shared ideas and practical therapy guidance.

Residential Services

Safe residential support for children from outside Mochudi.

Governance Structure

Motswedi Rehabilitation Centre operates under the Mother’s Union of the Dutch Reformed Church in Botswana.

The Centre is guided through structured leadership, ethical management and community accountability.

Governance focuses on transparency, inclusion, service delivery and long-term sustainability.

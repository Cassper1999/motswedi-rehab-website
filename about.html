WHO WE ARE

A fountain of hope,
dignity and empowerment.

Motswedi Rehabilitation Centre is a community-based rehabilitation centre serving children, youth and adults with disabilities through therapy, education, skills development and residential support.

Our Story

The Centre is a project of the Mother’s Union of the Dutch Reformed Church in Botswana – Mochudi Congregation.

Since opening in 1995, Motswedi has continued to provide rehabilitation, education and skills training services for people living with disabilities.

The Centre exists to restore dignity, promote independence and create opportunities for inclusion within the community.
